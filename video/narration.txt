PeakShift is an offline home load shifter. It plans appliance runs against synthetic hourly grid carbon intensity without accounts or external data.

Three profiles are included. Solar is cleanest at midday, wind overnight, and coal stays carbon intensive all day.

Each appliance has power, runtime, deadline, flexibility, circuit, and mode. One-shot loads stay contiguous. Repeatable loads may split.

The solver searches exact hour placements. It honors deadlines, never double-books a circuit, and draws the lowest-carbon schedule over curve.

Users must acknowledge the results are modeled, not measured, before the summary opens. The app names omissions including tariffs, rebound effects, embodied carbon, and efficiency losses.

The comparison is a naive evening-first plan. PeakShift reports both totals and marginal savings per appliance for identical energy demand.

PeakShift is not a control system or proof of reduction. It shows how timing can change emissions when the same work gets done.
